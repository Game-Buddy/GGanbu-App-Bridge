## Design Context

### Users

People running a GameBuddy bridge on a desktop who connect trusted devices over their local network to send game actions.

### Brand Personality

Operational, calm, precise. The interface should create confidence that the bridge and its clients are understandable and under control.

### Aesthetic Direction

Existing dark technical console style: dense, restrained, green status accents, clear dividers, and practical data presentation. Avoid decorative gradients, oversized cards, or playful consumer-app styling.

### Design Principles

- Make connection state immediately legible.
- Keep remote-device management direct and reversible where possible.
- Favor useful metadata over decorative UI.
- Preserve the compact desktop-console rhythm and support smaller screens.
