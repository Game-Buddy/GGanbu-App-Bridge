# Branding policy

"GGanbu", "GGanbu App Bridge", "Game Buddy", and the project logos identify the official project maintained by Game Buddy.

The GNU Affero General Public License governs the software and artwork as copyrighted works. It does not grant permission to present a modified build as an official Game Buddy release.

Forks may use the names and logos to explain their origin or compatibility when they:

- state clearly that the fork is modified and unofficial;
- name the fork's maintainer;
- do not claim endorsement, sponsorship, or support from Game Buddy; and
- do not use branding that could make users mistake the fork for an official download.

A clear notice such as "Unofficial fork of GGanbu App Bridge, modified and maintained by [name]" satisfies the first two requirements.

This policy governs use of the names and logos as source identifiers. It does not limit copying or modifying files under the repository's copyright license when the marks are not used to identify the source of a product or service.

Questions about uses outside this policy may be sent through the contact method listed in the repository.
