# Code of Conduct

GGanbu App Bridge should be a respectful, inclusive, and welcoming project for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Discuss ideas constructively and accept good-faith feedback.
- Focus on the project and its users, not on personal attacks.
- Respect privacy and do not share personal information without permission.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized language or attention, trolling, insults, unwanted contact, publishing private information, and other conduct that is inappropriate in a professional community are not allowed.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately through the [Game Buddy contact page](https://gganbu.app/contact). Include a description, relevant links or screenshots, and how you can be contacted safely. Do not post sensitive personal information publicly.

Report security vulnerabilities through the private process in [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md), not through an issue, discussion, or pull request. Use [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/Game-Buddy/GGanbu-App-Bridge/discussions) for support and general project questions.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical and reviewed fairly. Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, or temporarily or permanently ban participants for unacceptable behavior. They will explain decisions when it is safe and appropriate to do so.

This policy applies to the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and other official project spaces.
